Request Your Free Sample Edit and Manuscript Assessment

Managing Editor Debra L. Hartmann will reply to your email within 1 business day and request your manuscript Word document.

Email theprobookeditor@outlook.com

Copy the questionnaire below into your email, fill it out, and attach your manuscript Word document:

  • Your Name
  • Book Title
  • Genre
  • Word Count
  • US English or UK English? (For nonfiction works, please indicate US/UK and the additional style guide for your topic area, i.e. APA, medical journal submission requirements doc, etc.)
  • Book Description / Synopsis (even the roughest first draft is perfectly fine)
  • When will you be finished writing and self-editing and ready to begin working?
  • What kind of editing do you feel is needed?
  • Have you worked with an editor before? What are your expectations?
  • Have you used Track Changes before?
  • Will you be self-publishing or submitting your manuscript to agents/publishers?
