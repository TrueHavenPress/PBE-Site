About TPBE

Your writing deserves an editor who respects it.

Writing a manuscript is no small thing. Finding the right editor to polish it, someone who actually gets your vision, not someone who'll rewrite your voice out of existence? That can feel overwhelming.

At The Pro Book Editor, we keep it simple—you'll work directly with Senior Managing Editor Debra L. Hartmann to build an editing plan that fits your book, your budget, and your timeline. Behind her are two dozen hand-picked editing and proofreading professionals—no interns, no AI, no guesswork.

Debra's 25+ years of experience means she's seen it all and helped countless authors (many now award-winners and bestsellers) get their manuscripts publishing ready.

She personally customizes every editing plan to fit your manuscript and your goals. No cookie-cutter packages. No pressure tactics. No rushing you through. Just skilled professionals who care about your words as much as you do.
